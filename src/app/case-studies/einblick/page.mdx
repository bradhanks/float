## Executive Summary

Einblick, a seed-funded data science platform spun out of MIT and Brown University research, needed data engineers and data scientists to know it existed. We built a long-tail[^1] content program of how-to articles on individual Python functions, such as pandas groupby() and Python's next(). A Python pipeline using NLP[^2] libraries and custom scripts studied what the top-ranking pages covered for each function and turned that into structured drafts. The articles put Einblick in front of its audience at the top of the funnel[^3], while they were in the middle of writing code. By December 2023, monthly organic traffic had grown from about 3,000 visits to more than 40,000, and 60 of the 150 articles had reached page one of Google. Databricks announced its acquisition of Einblick in January 2024.[^4]

## Background

Einblick was founded in 2019 by researchers from MIT and Brown, and came out of stealth in December 2020 with $6 million in seed funding.[^5] Its product was a collaborative data science platform for working in Python and SQL, and it later added an AI assistant that wrote code and charts from plain-English prompts.[^4] We worked with Einblick from September 2022 to December 2023.

## The Problem

Data engineers rarely search for a new data platform. They search all day for how to do one specific thing in Python: group a table, tokenize text, plot a histogram. Each of those searches is small, but there are thousands of them, and documentation sites and coding blogs already compete for every one. A seed-stage team could not write that many accurate code tutorials by hand.

When we started, einblick.ai had about 40 indexed pages, mostly product and docs pages, and brought in roughly 3,000 organic visits a month. Content was handled part-time by the founding team in between product launches.

## The Approach

### Picking the functions

We started from the top 100 target keywords and read the top 20 ranking pages for each. Function-level searches like "pandas groupby agg" became the article list, weighted toward the keywords Einblick most wanted to win. We built the list in Ahrefs, pulling every function-level query for the major Python data libraries and filtering by search volume, keyword difficulty and how closely the task matched something Einblick could do.

### The pipeline

Python scripts collected the ranking pages, pulled out the subtopics and examples they covered, and turned that into prompts. Competitor pages were used to fine-tune[^6] the output so each draft covered what the top results covered. Scraping ran on requests and BeautifulSoup, spaCy handled topic extraction, and drafts were generated with OpenAI's GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 models.

### The library

The articles went live under einblick.ai/python-code-examples, covering pandas, NumPy, SciPy, seaborn, matplotlib, statsmodels, scikit-learn and NLTK.[^7] Each follows one shape: a short intro, a real dataset, then a run of worked code examples with their output, ending with a pitch to try Einblick. About 150 articles went live between March and August 2023. Einblick's data scientists ran every code example and checked the output before an article was published.

## Results

- Monthly organic traffic grew from about 3,000 visits to more than 40,000 between the first articles going live and December 2023.
- 60 of the 150 function articles reached page one of Google, including the guides to pandas groupby() with agg() and Python's next().
- The articles drove roughly 1,200 new Einblick signups, making organic search the largest single source of new accounts.

On January 30, 2024, Databricks announced it would acquire Einblick and fold its AI-native approach into the Databricks platform. Terms were not disclosed.[^4] By the time of the deal, the code examples library was the most visited section of einblick.ai and the main way new users found the product.
